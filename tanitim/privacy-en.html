Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

← ViraCheck · KVKK (Türkçe) · Türkçe oku →

ViraCheck is an iPhone/iPad app and a website (viracheck.com.tr) that brings boat charter and tour destinations, your pre-departure checks, logbook and marine weather together in one place. This policy explains what data the app and the web version touch and what they do with it. An Android app has not been released; on Android devices the web version is used.

In short

  • ViraCheck has no accounts — you never sign up.
  • Everything you create — checklists, logbook, boat details — stays on your device.
  • There are no ad networks, and we never sell your data.
  • We collect anonymous usage counts (how many boats, how many trips). Your name, your location and your logbook content are never included, it is on by default and you can turn it off at any time inside the app (see section 6).

1. Data stored on your device

The following is kept only in your device's local storage (in the web version, your browser's local storage):

  • Checklists and the items you tick off
  • Logbook entries (departure and arrival port and time, distance, engine hours, fuel, crew, weather note, route notes)
  • Boat details
  • App settings

This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us. If iCloud backup is enabled on your device, this content may be included in Apple's backup, in which case Apple's privacy policy applies to it. In the web version, clearing your browser data also deletes this content; it is not synced to other devices or browsers.

2. Location

We ask for location permission so the app can calculate wind, wave, and go/no-go information for where you actually are. The permission is requested through your operating system's (in the web version, your browser's) own prompt when you first open the app. In the web version you can also pick a region from a list instead; the region you pick is stored only in your browser.

  • Your precise location is not stored on your device, and no location history is kept.
  • Your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap only while a forecast or cove request is being made.
  • So the nearby cove list is not re-fetched every time, an approximate location (about 1 km resolution) is cached on your device alongside the results. This cache is capped at 12 areas, never leaves your device, and is removed by Reset data.
  • To display the name of your location on screen, the coordinates are passed to a geocoding service: Apple's (through the operating system) on iPhone and iPad, and OpenStreetMap's Nominatim in the web version.
  • The map on the route screen is drawn with Apple Maps on iPhone and iPad and with OpenStreetMap map tiles in the web version. Because map imagery is downloaded from these services, the area the map shows is passed to that service.
  • If you decline the permission, the rest of the app works normally — only the weather and sea state cannot be shown.
  • You can revoke the permission at any time in your device's Settings (in the web version, your browser's site settings).

3. Weather, sea state and cove data

Forecast data comes from Open-Meteo. These requests carry only coordinates and the requested forecast fields; nothing identifying you is sent. Open-Meteo's terms: open-meteo.com/en/terms

The nearby cove and beach list comes from OpenStreetMap data via the Overpass API. These requests likewise carry only your coordinates and the search radius; no account or identifying information is sent.

The list of Blue Flag beaches, coves and marinas is bundled inside the app: this feature makes no network requests at all, and your location is never sent anywhere for it. The list is compiled from data published by the Foundation for Environmental Education; ViraCheck is not affiliated with that organisation.

The viracheck.com.tr website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When pages are served, technical connection details such as your IP address may be processed by GitHub for security and to operate the service (GitHub privacy statement). The site uses no advertising or tracking cookies; fonts and images are loaded from the site itself.

4. Boat charter and tour links

The cards in the Charter tab lead to partner platforms' regional pages — both platforms where you rent the whole boat and platforms selling guided boat tours. ViraCheck does not sell charters or tours, take bookings, or act as an intermediary.

  • When you tap a card, the link opens in an in-app browser (in the web version, a new tab) and from that point the partner's privacy policy applies. Membership, payment and the charter contract are established there.
  • The partners we work with: SamBoat, SkipperCity, Viravira (boat charter) and GetYourGuide (boat tours).
  • We send the partner nothing that identifies you; the link carries only an affiliate identifier indicating it came from ViraCheck. The partner may set cookies on its own site.
  • For some partners (SamBoat) the redirect passes through the Awin affiliate network's domain; Viravira links pass through the partner's own short-link domain; SkipperCity and GetYourGuide links only carry our affiliate identifier. At that step the network may set its own cookie to record which publisher the click belongs to. We send these networks nothing that identifies you either.
  • We may earn a commission on charters booked through these links. This does not change the price you see.
  • On the app side we only count how many times and which regional card was tapped, and even that is sent only while the measurement described in section 6 is switched on.
  • Organiser links: if you arrived through a link shared by a tour organiser or partner (with ?o=CODE in the address), that code is stored in your browser for up to 30 days and added to charter/tour links as a campaign tag, so a referral can be attributed to that organiser. The code identifies the link, not you; while the measurement is on, the number of visits and taps from that code is counted.

5. Subscriptions and purchases

The ViraCheck Pro subscription is sold through the App Store on iPhone and iPad (there are no purchases in the web version) and managed with RevenueCat.

  • Your payment details are handled by Apple and are never shared with us.
  • To track your subscription status, RevenueCat processes purchase information (product, date, renewal state) together with technical details such as device type and country, under a randomly generated anonymous user ID. Your name and email address are not sent.
  • RevenueCat privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple Media Services privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store

6. Anonymous usage statistics

To understand where the app needs work, we collect anonymous usage counts. The measurement is on by default and you can turn it off at any time; once it is off, not a single request is sent.

While it is on, the following is sent at most once a day:

  • How many boats and trips you have recorded, total nautical miles, how many checklists you completed
  • How many times you tapped a charter link, and which regional card; if you arrived through an organiser link, that link's code and the number of visits/taps from it
  • How often each tab was opened, how many times the app was launched, how many times the subscription screen was shown and how many times its purchase button was tapped
  • Whether you have a Pro subscription, the app version, platform (iOS or web) and interface language
  • In the web version: how many times the site was opened that day
  • The first and last date this installation was seen

In addition, while the app is open, a last-active timestamp is written at most once a minute. It exists to answer "how many installations are using the app right now" and carries no other data; nothing is sent at all while the measurement is off.

Never sent: your name, your email, boat name, MMSI, call sign, your trip notes, port and cove names, your location, or what you searched for. Your logbook and boat details stay on your device.

Records are tied to an identifier generated at random for this installation. It is not linked to your Apple Account, device serial number, or advertising identifier; if you delete and reinstall the app (in the web version, clear your browser data), the link is broken and a new identifier is created. If you restore your phone from an iCloud backup, the identifier comes back along with the app's data; switching the measurement off is enough to break the link. In other words we count installations, not people, and we do not track users across apps or devices.

The data is stored on Google's Firebase infrastructure in the European Union region (eur3 — Belgium and the Netherlands). Only the ViraCheck developer can access it; it is not shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising. Google's privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis for this measurement is our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f)); you can object at any time simply by switching it off. You can turn it off in the app under Boat → Notifications → Anonymous statistics. Turning it off deletes the measurement identifier and counters from your device and stops any further sending.

7. Notifications

Notification permission is requested through your operating system's own prompt when you first open the app. Granting it does not switch any notification on by itself; it is used only once you turn on the good-conditions reminder. You can withdraw it at any time in your device settings.

The app uses only local notifications that run on your device (for example, a good-conditions reminder). Remote push infrastructure is disabled in the app; no data is sent to any server in order to deliver a notification.

8. Children's privacy

ViraCheck is not directed at users under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Deleting your data

You can reset your checklists and boat details from the relevant sections inside the app. To permanently erase all local data, simply remove the app from your device, or in the web version clear this site's data in your browser.

Turning off the anonymous statistics switch deletes the measurement identifier from your device. If you want anonymous records already sent to be deleted, write to the address below; because records are tied to an installation identifier, you may need to provide yours. You may also ask us to delete the record entirely.

You can manage or cancel your subscription on iPhone and iPad in Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Subscription records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change. For significant changes, we will also notify you inside the app.
