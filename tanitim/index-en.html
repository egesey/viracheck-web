FAQ

Questions
people ask

Is ViraCheck free?

Yes. Charter and tour destinations, wind and sea state, the go/no-go call and the distress screen are all free. Pro lifts the limits: a 7-day forecast, every nearby cove, an unlimited logbook and PDF export.

Does ViraCheck charter the boat?

No. ViraCheck lists destinations; tapping one opens our partner’s booking page (SamBoat, SkipperCity, Viravira, GetYourGuide). Price, payment, contract and cancellation are with the partner. We may earn a commission; your price does not change.

Do I need an account?

No. ViraCheck never asks for an account, an email or a name. Your checklists, logbook and boat details stay on your own device.

Can I use it on Android or a computer?

The app is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. On Android or a computer, use the browser version at viracheck.com.tr — the same features are there.

Where does the weather data come from?

Wind, waves and sea state come from Open-Meteo, coves and beaches from OpenStreetMap, Blue Flag data from FEE. ViraCheck is an aid; it does not replace official maritime warnings or the skipper’s judgement.

Which countries have charters?

Türkiye, Greece, Croatia, Italy, Spain, France, Portugal, Montenegro, Malta, Monaco, the USA and Dubai — 93 destinations in 12 countries, ranked by how far they are from you.
