Peace of mind

Your questions.

What guests ask most before booking. Anything else? Write to us.

Who handles the booking?

ViraCheck does not own or charter yachts; we help you find the right one. Yachts in Türkiye open on Viravira, other countries on SamBoat and SkipperCity, and boat tours on GetYourGuide. Booking, payment and confirmation are handled directly by these partners.

Is payment secure?

You pay on the partner’s own secure payment page. ViraCheck never sees or stores your card details.

Does booking through ViraCheck change the price?

No. The partner may pay us a commission for bookings made through our links; not a cent is added to what you pay.

Are the captain, fuel and meals included?

It depends on the yacht. Every listing clearly states whether it comes with a captain, a full crew or bareboat, and exactly what the price includes; you can check it on the listing before you book.

Can I cancel?

Cancellation and change terms are shown on each listing and follow the partner’s policy. We recommend reading them before you book.

Do I need an account?

No ViraCheck account is needed. When you complete the booking, the partner only asks for the details the booking requires.
