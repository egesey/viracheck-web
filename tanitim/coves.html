Before you anchor

A peaceful night in the cove.

Four habits that turn a beautiful cove into a good anchorage.

  • Put your back to the wind

    If the cove’s mouth faces the wind, the swell rolls in. Choose a cove closed by land on the windward side, and allow for the wind shifting at night.

  • Never anchor in seagrass

    Posidonia meadows keep the water clear and grow very slowly. Drop the anchor on sand and never drag chain across the meadow.

  • Be generous with chain

    Let out at least three to five times the depth in chain and test the hold in reverse. If you take a line ashore, do not damage trees or rocks.

  • Give way to swimmers

    Do not motor into buoyed swimming areas or in front of Blue Flag beaches; slow down inside the cove and watch for swimmers.
